The Marketplace Laboratory from class without the group box, the lab book and the class record. Build the market in the question, run the policy, read off the numbers. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; reload the page for a clean start.

How a question becomes rows

Each row of the Supply or Demand table is a group of units whose costs (sellers) or values (buyers) are spread evenly from Pmin to Pmax. Rows with the same region name belong to one region; a new name makes a new region.

  • Demand. Pmax is the highest price any buyer in the group would pay, Pmin the price at which every buyer in the group buys, and Quantity the total demand at Pmin. Q buyers who all value the good at v correspond to Pmin = Pmax = v and Quantity = Q.
  • Supply. Pmin is the lowest cost in the group, Pmax the highest, and Quantity the group's total capacity. Producers with the same marginal cost up to an aggregate capacity correspond to Pmin = Pmax = the marginal cost and Quantity = the capacity.
  • Policies. A tariff on imports from a country, when all buyers are at home: a tax on that supply region under Regional experiments. A tax on buyers or sellers: Global experiments. A price ceiling or floor: Price and quantity controls, with the rationing rule the question states.
